# Demo changelog covers

Six original illustrations generated with the built-in imagegen tool.

Shared prompt: Landscape 3:2 featured illustration for a Tandora SaaS changelog card. Friendly clean editorial illustration with soft dimensional shading. White background, generous whitespace, sky blue and indigo #284BB0 with pale lavender #EDF0FA and subtle teal accents. Large simple shapes readable at thumbnail size. Professional and playful. No text, letters, watermark, screenshot, collage or sheet.

Subject prompts:
- welcome.png: Blue announcement drum, two drumsticks and celebratory confetti.
- editor.png: Rich-text editor, abstract text lines, picture tile, formatting controls and blue fountain pen.
- categories.png: Three stacks of rounded category cards with blue, teal and violet tabs and geometric symbols.
- public.png: Browser with release-note cards, a globe and orbiting connection dots.
- links.png: Central profile card connected to four link cards with chain-link symbols and arrows.
- improvements.png: Blue gear, checkmark badge, wrench and rising progress bars.

`python manage.py seed_demo` attaches these to demo posts without replacing an existing featured image.
